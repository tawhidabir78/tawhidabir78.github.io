What files will I receive?

The formats your work needs: PNG, PDF and vector files for design; print-ready PDFs in CMYK with 3mm bleed for print; and layered DWG files plus scaled PDFs for AutoCAD — ready to publish, print or build from.

Do you work in Bengali and English?

Yes — designs, captions, voiceovers and this whole site switch between Bengali and English. Use the EN / বাংলা buttons at the top.

Can you redraw an old or hand-sketched plan?

Yes. Old, scanned or hand-sketched plans are redrawn cleanly in AutoCAD, with proper dimensions and separate layers for walls, electrical work and dimensions — so the next engineer can edit one system without breaking the rest.

Can you run a Facebook page end-to-end?

Yes. Pages run on a monthly content calendar, with graphics and videos produced in batches and scheduled ahead, and Messenger answering first contact and common questions automatically — so the page sells even when nobody is watching it.
